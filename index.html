Complete series guide

Sakamoto Days Manga: Story, Anime, Characters and Ratings

Sakamoto Days is an action-comedy manga written and illustrated by Yuto Suzuki. Taro Sakamoto was once feared as an unmatched hitman, but leaves that life after falling in love, marries Aoi, starts a family, and runs a neighborhood shop. Former colleagues and new assassins repeatedly bring danger to his peaceful routine. Shin Asakura and other allies help protect the people Sakamoto cares about.

Genre and style

The series blends action, comedy, crime, and slice-of-life moments. Its appeal comes from the contrast between Sakamoto’s ordinary shopkeeper routine and extraordinary combat skills. The family and friendship stories give the action emotional stakes.

Does Sakamoto Days have an anime?

Yes. The TV anime premiered in 2025 and is available internationally through Netflix. A second season has been announced for January 2027. Check official announcements for schedule changes and streaming details.

Sakamoto Days ratings

Scores differ between the manga, first anime cour, and second cour. An August 2026 snapshot listed the first anime season at about 7.59/10 on MyAnimeList and the second cour around 7.91/10. Community ratings change as people vote, and manga scores are separate.
